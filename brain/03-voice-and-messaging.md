# Voice, Messaging and Compliance Rules

Last updated: 2026-11-05. Drawn from the live consultant sales page (becomeanaiconsultant.com/joinus) and the November brief. Refine with the summit copy doc when it arrives.

## Voice

- **Calm, credible, professional.** Short declarative headlines with an italic second line ("Businesses are already looking for AI help. *Become the person they call.*").
- **Honest about limits.** The live page has a "This may NOT be for you if…" block (no guaranteed income formula, no one hands you clients, no "get rich with AI"). Keep that tone.
- **Your experience counts:** "You don't have to start as an AI expert." AI is the new capability; existing expertise is the foundation.
- Stats always carry a named source (WEF, CompTIA, Grand View Research). The summit copy doc includes sources too; keep them.
- **Summit angle (new):** AI in everyday life, practical and useful, not business/income-led.

## Signature lines (reuse)

- "AI should empower people." (Alicia)
- "You don't need to be a technical AI expert. You need your experience, your curiosity, and a willingness to practice."
- "From 'I know AI' to 'I advise on AI.'"
- "Creating opportunity through AI, not simply efficiency." (AI InnoVision mission)

## Hard compliance rules (non-negotiable)

1. **No income, client or "guaranteed" promises.** Education only.
2. **Testimonials:** real, named, approved ones only; prefer the speaking/visibility wins already in use; earnings disclaimer in the footer.
3. **Values and prices must be verified** (brief: "verified total value"). Never stack made-up values. Don't promise unconfirmed bonuses.
4. **Deadlines:** "November 30 at 11:59 p.m. PT" everywhere; summit "November 18–19, 1 p.m. ET".
5. **Footer on every page:** © AI InnoVision, legal links (one set), earnings disclaimer, Meta non-affiliation line.
6. **SMS consent** whenever a phone number is collected.
7. **No politics.** (The White House / EPA career line is fine as career history.)
8. Thanksgiving meetup: no selling beyond one light book-a-call line.
