# Design System: Alicia Lyttle / AI InnoVision Funnels

Last updated: 2026-11-05. Measured from the live consultant funnel (becomeanaiconsultant.com), desktop. Two styles are live. The summit will follow the design canvas Alicia's team sends (⛔ not received); re-measure from that.

## Style A: new long-form sales page (/joinus): navy + gold, editorial

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--navy-950` | ~`#0B1528` | Hero and dark sections, H2 text on light |
| `--gold-400` | ~`#C9A06A` | Italic accent headline line, buttons, play button |
| `--white` | `#FFFFFF` | H1 on dark |

- **Display:** Playfair Display, H1 54px/500, H2 46px/600; second headline line in gold italic.
- **Body / UI:** Inter.
- **Buttons:** gold gradient, navy text, rounded, arrow icon ("Enroll Now →").
- **Patterns:** sticky top nav with anchor links; centered VSL in a rounded card; small uppercase proof strip under the headline; numbered lists; value-stack table; FAQ accordion.

## Style B: GHL order / thank-you / upsell pages: navy + mustard, bold caps

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--navy-900` | `#082745` | H1/H2 text, "what you'll get" card |
| `--mustard-400` | ~`#D4A853` | Headline gradient, tabs, buttons |
| `--cream-50` | ~`#FBF4E6` | Page background with circuit-line art |

- **Fonts:** Stack Sans Headline SemiBold (uppercase headlines), Stack Sans Text ExtraLight (body), Anton (some labels).
- **Patterns:** 2-step order form (Your Information → Payment Info) with shipping block; navy "What you'll get inside" checklist card; pay-in-full bonus box; card logos under the form.
