Why This Isn't Another AI Webinar
Most free AI events are a demo and a pitch. You watch someone else's screen, write down three tools, and nothing in your life changes by Wednesday.
This is two days of building, on your stuff. The method is called  Teach It Your Life , and it has three steps:
1
Brain dump.
You tell the assistant about your life the way you'd tell a friend: who's in your house, what's on your plate, what you're tired of. My 90-second Brain Dump to Super Prompt formula turns that into instructions it understands. You never type from a blank box.
2
Build.
We set up one assistant that remembers all of it. Not a new app. The free ChatGPT or Claude account on the phone already in your hand.
3
Hand off.
One at a time, you give it the 12 jobs below. Each one takes minutes once it knows you.
You can't get this from a YouTube video, because a video doesn't know your family. Your assistant will.

The 12 Jobs Your Assistant Takes Over
You build one assistant on Day 1. Then you hand it these, one at a time, live with me on screen.
Your Home & Family
Your inbox, your texts and your calendar.  Sorts the email and the texts you forgot to answer, drafts the replies, unsubscribes you from the junk, and keeps one calendar for the whole house: the school app, the group chats, the practices, the hair appointment.
Dinner.  A week of meals around your budget and your picky eaters, grocery list written.
Thanksgiving and the holidays.  Menu, shopping list, the who-brings-what text, gift list on a budget. Thanksgiving is nine days after the summit.
The people you take care of.  Homework help that explains instead of just handing over the answer. A system for a parent's appointments, forms and calls.
Your Money
Your budget.  Built from your real expenses, forgotten subscriptions flagged, a plan to pay down the card.
Your bills.  A word-for-word script to call and lower the cable, phone and insurance bills.
Your credit.  Read the report line by line, write the dispute and goodwill letters, and build a month-by-month plan to raise the score before you need it.
The paperwork pile.  Medical bills, leases, the mechanic's estimate, insurance claims, letters to the school or HOA, tax-season receipts.
Your Income & Your Trip
Extra income.  A side hustle that fits your skills and your real hours, with the offer, the price and your first three customers mapped out. Or your case for a raise, written, with the numbers.
The trip, the reunion, the girls' weekend.  Dates, deals, a day-by-day itinerary on your budget, the packing list, and the weekly savings plan to pay for it in cash.
You & Your 2027
Your health routine.  Meals, movement and sleep that fit your real week, plus the questions for your next doctor's visit so you walk in prepared and walk out heard.
Your 2027 roadmap.  One goal that matters, broken into monthly steps, with the first step on your calendar before you log off.
Every one of these starts the same way: my 90-second Brain Dump to Super Prompt formula. You talk to it the way you talk. It does the rest.
